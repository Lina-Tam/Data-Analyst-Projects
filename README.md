What it is: A sales database for an online store.
I built the tables, loaded sample data, and wrote 20 queries to answer common business questions.

Database tables: users (customers), products, categories, orders,order_items, and product_reviews

Business questions answered:

Total sales by product and by category
Which products were never ordered, or are out of stock
Which customers placed the most orders, or no orders at all
Average days between order date and delivery date
Most recent review for each product
Reviews that mention specific words, or were posted in a certain month

SQL skills used: JOIN and LEFT JOIN, subqueries, GROUP BY, SUM, COUNT, AVG, LIKE, DATEDIFF, PRIMARY KEY / FOREIGN KEY, CHECK constraints
